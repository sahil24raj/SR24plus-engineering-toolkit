# PRODUCT ENGINEERING TOOLKIT

> **Domain Identifier**: `11-PRODUCT-ENGINEERING` • **Registered Skills**: `212` • **Trigger Command**: `"Activate Product Engineering Toolkit"` (/sr24 product)

Product management, sprint planning, feature prioritization, Jira delivery workflows, market opportunity discovery, and strategic sales execution.

---

## Autonomous Multi-Agent Workflow Pipeline

When this toolkit is activated, Antigravity coordinates the following specialized roles sequentially:

### Phase 1: Opportunity Discovery
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-strategy-playbooks-phase-0-discovery`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Validate problem-market fit, competitor battlecards, and user willingness to pay.

### Phase 2: Product Spec & Requirements
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `spec-driven-development` + `agency-product-manager`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Author detailed Product Requirement Documents (PRDs), user stories, and acceptance criteria.

### Phase 3: Sprint Prioritization
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-product-sprint-prioritizer` + `planning-and-task-breakdown`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Apply RICE/MoSCoW frameworks to break epics into actionable developer tasks.

### Phase 4: Jira & Git Workflow Delivery
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-project-management-jira-workflow-steward`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Enforce traceable commit messages, branch naming conventions, and release-ready pull requests.

### Phase 5: Go-to-Market & Sales Alignment
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-sales-engineer` + `agency-sales-coach`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Scope technical POCs, sales demo architectures, and customer discovery questionnaires.
