# RESEARCH AND ANALYTICS TOOLKIT

> **Domain Identifier**: `09-RESEARCH-AND-ANALYTICS` • **Registered Skills**: `91` • **Trigger Command**: `"Activate Research & Analytics Toolkit"` (/sr24 research)

Scientific research methodology, quantitative data synthesis, statistical inference, spatial GIS analysis, and market intelligence.

---

## Autonomous Multi-Agent Workflow Pipeline

When this toolkit is activated, Antigravity coordinates the following specialized roles sequentially:

### Phase 1: Literature & Source Review
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-research-synthesist`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Collect, filter, and structure peer-reviewed and primary industry literature.

### Phase 2: Quantitative Methodology
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-academic-statistician`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Design experimental controls, sample power calculations, hypothesis testing, and p-value validation.

### Phase 3: Spatial & GIS Modeling
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-gis-spatial-data-scientist` + `agency-academic-geographer`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Perform geospatial vector/raster analysis, spatial clustering, and terrain analysis.

### Phase 4: Behavioral & Cultural Analysis
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-academic-anthropologist` + `agency-academic-psychologist`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Evaluate human motivation, cultural frameworks, and user behavioral patterns.

### Phase 5: Executive Synthesis & Reporting
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-support-analytics-reporter`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Transform complex findings into decision-ready executive dashboards and data visualizations.
