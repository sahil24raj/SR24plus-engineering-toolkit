## 4-PHASE RESEARCH PROTOCOL

1. **Phase 1: Hypothesis & Problem Formulation**:
   - Frame specific, answerable research questions.
   - Define search keywords, inclusion criteria, and exclusion boundaries.
2. **Phase 2: Multi-Source Evidence Gathering**:
   - Query primary sources (academic papers, official docs, databases, live benchmarks).
   - Document DOIs, PMCIDs, CIDs, or validated web citations.
3. **Phase 3: Evidence Weighting & Bias Elimination**:
   - Separate verified empirical facts from marketing claims or anecdotal opinions.
   - Run statistical checks against sample size limitations and selection bias.
4. **Phase 4: Synthesis & Actionable Deliverable**:
   - Synthesize findings into structured, decision-ready reports:
     - Executive Summary
     - Evidence Matrix
     - Risk & Tradeoff Analysis
     - Concrete Technical Recommendations
