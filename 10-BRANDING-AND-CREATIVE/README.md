# BRANDING AND CREATIVE TOOLKIT

> **Domain Identifier**: `10-BRANDING-AND-CREATIVE` • **Registered Skills**: `45` • **Trigger Command**: `"Activate Branding & Creative Toolkit"` (/sr24 creative)

Brand strategy, editorial content creation, visual storytelling, narrative architecture, and multi-channel campaign engagement.

---

## Autonomous Multi-Agent Workflow Pipeline

When this toolkit is activated, Antigravity coordinates the following specialized roles sequentially:

### Phase 1: Strategic Brand Positioning
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-design-brand-guardian`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Formulate brand archetype, core value proposition, tone of voice, and brand guidelines.

### Phase 2: Narrative & Story Structure
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-academic-narratologist` + `agency-design-visual-storyteller`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Construct cohesive brand mythologies, character journeys, and visual narratives.

### Phase 3: Editorial & Multimodal Copy
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-marketing-content-creator`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Author high-impact headlines, editorial calendars, long-form copy, and press releases.

### Phase 4: Social Media Campaigns
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-marketing-twitter-engager` + `agency-marketing-instagram-curator`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Craft viral threads, carousel posts, visual aesthetics, and community engagement loops.

### Phase 5: Delight & Personality
- **Primary Autonomous Role**: `agency-design-whimsy-injector`
- **Responsibilities & Output**: Inject unexpected moments of delight, micro-copy humor, and memorable brand easter eggs.
